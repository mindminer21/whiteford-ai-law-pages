# Controlling content-framing override

**Status: active and controlling.**

Jeff has retired the generic “readiness file,” “proof file,” “evidence file,” “diligence file,” “control file,” “operating file,” “launch file,” folder, dossier, data-room, proof-stack, and document-bundle motif.

This rule overrides older dated packets, LinkedIn drafts, authority-angle documents, strategy examples, QMD search results, deliverable-index entries, and prior session outputs that recommend or model that framing.

## Required approach

- Start with the current source and the reader’s actual business decision, legal consequence, operating tradeoff, or next action.
- Do not make assembling records or documents the thesis, hook, artifact, CTA, lead magnet, visual metaphor, or conclusion.
- Do not preserve the same concept under a synonym or adjacent folder/dossier/map/room metaphor.
- Mention an actual file, record, or data room only when Jeff expressly asks or a verified legal, regulatory, contractual, or transaction-specific requirement independently makes it necessary.
- When an actual record is relevant, call it by its precise ordinary name and explain why it matters; do not brand a new reusable “file” framework.

## Better content forms

Choose the form that fits the subject: decision tree, comparison, action sequence, issue checklist, calculator, worksheet, annotated product flow, risk matrix, constraint map, scenario analysis, or direct answers to the source-supported questions.
