Risk gates

Group the approval matrix into five gates Jeff can scan fast.

Identity

Auth + client identity + conflicts

Who may enter, which organization/matter exists, whether engaged-client/conflicts/matter-scope status authorizes portal use, and who can deprovision access.

Data

Documents + messages + retention

Where content lives, what class of data is allowed, malware/access/retention rules, and whether messages/documents can become records.

Action

Routing + scheduling + notifications

What a tag means, whether anyone is notified, what request-only means, what calendar system is allowed, and what copy avoids promises.

External systems

AI + CRM + Supabase/Clio/DMS

Whether any model, marketing record, matter system, system of record, sync, schema, or analytics path can touch portal data.

Professional-responsibility gate

Disclaimers and stop rules are not garnish

Emergency, confidentiality, no-advice, no-engagement-expansion, no-availability, and approved-client-only copy must be exact and placed at submission moments.

Business-development gate

Keep the promise concrete and bounded

The portal should make the OGC package feel like managed access and recurring counsel, not unlimited service, auto-lawyer assignment, or compliance certification.
