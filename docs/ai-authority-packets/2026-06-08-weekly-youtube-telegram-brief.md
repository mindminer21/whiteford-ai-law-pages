## 1) AI Patent Brief — “The AI tool is not the inventor: what founders should document”

**Primary keyword:** `ai-assisted invention`  
**Search intent:** Founder/inventor wants to know whether AI use destroys patent options and what to document before filing or diligence.  
**Title options:**
1. `Can You Patent an AI-Assisted Invention? The Human Contribution File`
2. `AI Inventorship in 2026: What Founders Should Document Before Filing`
3. `Using AI in R&D? Build This Patent Record Before the Demo Gets Copied`

**Thumbnail hook:** `AI helped. Who invented?`

**6-part outline:**
1. Cold open: “The question is not just ‘did AI help?’ — it is what human contribution can be shown.”
2. What USPTO guidance generally says: AI tools do not become human inventors; avoid turning this into a case-specific inventorship conclusion.
3. The founder risk: prompts, prototypes, model outputs, experiments, and claim ideas can blur the invention record if no one keeps notes.
4. The Human Contribution File: conception notes, technical problem/solution, alternatives rejected, experiment logs, architecture diagrams, and ownership/assignment records.
5. Patent strategy triage: patent vs trade secret, eligibility framing, vendor/model/data ownership, contractor assignment hygiene.
6. CTA: run the AI Patent Strategy Scorecard and bring one non-confidential invention workflow to a strategy discussion.
