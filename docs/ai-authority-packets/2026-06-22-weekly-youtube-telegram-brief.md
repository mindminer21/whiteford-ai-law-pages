- **Thumbnail hook:** `AI ≠ INVENTOR. YOUR RECORD MATTERS.`
- **6-part outline:**
  1. Cold open: “The risky question is not whether AI touched the work — it is whether the human contribution is documented.”

  6. CTA: run the scorecard before sending confidential details to anyone or filing under pressure.
