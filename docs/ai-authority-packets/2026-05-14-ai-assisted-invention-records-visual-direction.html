AI helped build it. Can the record explain the human invention story?

A restrained executive diagnostic board for AI/ML, robotics, autonomy, SaaS, cyber, and data teams preparing non-confidential questions before patent filing, diligence, or a patent-versus-trade-secret strategy decision.

Review posture Internal/private artifact only. Attorney, ethics, brand, and source-current review required before any public use.
1

AI role

  • Generated candidates, options, code, text, designs, or analysis
  • Optimized parameters, tested outputs, or summarized prior work
  • Helped classify, label, compare, search, or refine technical material

Visual treatment: quiet blue ledger column, not a futuristic “AI magic” panel.

2

Human contribution

  • Framed the technical problem and selected the design path
  • Made architecture, method, training, or implementation choices
  • Reviewed, modified, validated, rejected, or combined AI outputs

Visual treatment: sage decision column showing judgment, selection, and validation.

3

Record evidence

  • Notebooks, prompts, outputs, commits, tests, diagrams, and disclosures
  • Decision logs, ownership documents, assignments, and contributor history
  • Confidential details screened before public forms, comments, or email replies

Visual treatment: warm gold evidence column with paper-trail restraint.

Lead-magnet framing

The AI Patent Strategy Scorecard should feel like a preparation tool: identify human-contribution and record gaps before asking counsel to evaluate inventorship, ownership, patentability, filing strategy, or trade-secret treatment.
