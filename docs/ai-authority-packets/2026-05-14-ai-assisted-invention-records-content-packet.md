## 1. Content angle / executive hook

Working title: AI Helped Build It. Can You Prove the Human Invention Story?

Executive hook:

If your team used AI during R&D, the first patent-strategy question is not whether AI touched the work. It is whether the human contribution to conception is clear enough to explain, document, and align with the filing strategy.

Founder-facing angle:

AI assistance is not the end of patent strategy. The avoidable problem is a messy invention record: unclear human contribution, unclear AI role, missing prompt/output history, incomplete assignments, and no clean story for counsel to evaluate.

Practical diagnostic:

Before asking “can we patent this?”, ask whether the team can map five non-confidential items:

1. What technical problem was the team solving?
2. Which human contributors made meaningful technical choices?
3. What did the AI system suggest, generate, optimize, classify, summarize, or test?
4. Which records support the timeline: notebooks, prompts, commits, architecture diagrams, test results, invention disclosures, assignment documents, or decision logs?
5. What details should stay confidential until counsel reviews the filing and trade-secret strategy?

CTA posture:

Use the AI Patent Strategy Scorecard as a preparation tool. Invite the reader to issue-spot human-contribution and record gaps before a consultation, not to disclose confidential invention details in a public form.
