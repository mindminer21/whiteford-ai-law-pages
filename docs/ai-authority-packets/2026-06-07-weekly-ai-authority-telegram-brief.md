### 1) AI Patent Brief — The AI Invention File: prove the human contribution before you file
- **Primary keyword:** `USPTO AI inventorship guidance` / `AI-assisted invention patent`
- **Search intent:** Founder/inventor wants to know whether AI use breaks patent rights and what documentation to keep before filing.
- **Title options:**
  1. `If AI Helped Build It, Who Is the Inventor? The Founder’s Patent Checklist`
  2. `Can You Patent an AI-Assisted Invention? The Human Contribution Rule`
  3. `The AI Invention File: What to Document Before You File a Patent`
- **Thumbnail hook:** `AI helped. Who invented?`
- **6-part outline:**
  1. Cold open: AI use is not the fatal fact; undocumented human contribution is the problem.
  2. What USPTO guidance is actually asking: identify human inventors and human contribution.
  3. The “AI Invention File”: prompts, design choices, experiments, prototypes, alternatives rejected, and technical problem solved.
  4. Where founders get into trouble: AI-written disclosures, hallucinated embodiments, contractor/vendor ownership, and missing assignments.
  5. What to do before filing: privileged review, invention disclosure cleanup, claim-support map, trade secret vs. patent decision.
  6. CTA: run the scorecard before sending confidential details anywhere public.
- **Cited source anchors:**
  - USPTO AI resources hub — current AI inventorship/SME resources: https://www.uspto.gov/initiatives/artificial-intelligence/artificial-intelligence-resources
  - USPTO AI-assisted inventorship FAQ — human-contribution focus: https://www.uspto.gov/initiatives/artificial-intelligence/faqs
  - USPTO revised inventorship guidance — AI tools do not qualify as inventors: https://www.uspto.gov/subscription-center/2025/revised-inventorship-guidance-ai-assisted-inventions
- **Landing page CTA:** `/ai-patent-strategy-scorecard` — “Run the AI Patent Strategy Scorecard before filing or investor diligence.”
- **Lead magnet:** AI Patent Strategy Scorecard
- **HighLevel campaign tag:** `workflow:ai-patent-strategy`
- **Suggested email subject:** `If AI helped invent it, document this before filing`
- **2 Shorts cuts:**
  1. “AI can help invent, but it can’t be listed as the inventor.”

- **Legal/ethics caveat:** Educational only; do not state that a specific invention is patentable, that any person is/is not an inventor, or that AI use creates/defeats rights without confidential review, conflicts clearance, and a written engagement.
