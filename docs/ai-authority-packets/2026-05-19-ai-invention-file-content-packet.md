## 1. Content Angle + Executive Hook

**Angle:** The issue for AI-assisted invention is not "AI use kills patents." The practical executive risk is that the company cannot later explain the human contribution, tool role, ownership chain, and protection strategy when counsel, investors, enterprise customers, or diligence teams ask.

**Executive hook:** Before an AI-assisted R&D result becomes a patent filing, demo, fundraising story, or diligence exhibit, leadership should be able to produce an **AI Invention File**: a concise record of the technical problem, human conception decisions, AI tool use, IP ownership chain, and patent/trade-secret strategy posture.

## 2. LinkedIn Post Draft (900-1400 characters)

“We used AI in R&D” is not the patent problem. The problem is when no one can explain, months later, which human made the inventive choices.

The USPTO’s current posture is useful for executives: AI tools can assist invention, but named inventors need to be human beings. The analysis remains fact-specific, and an AI lab notebook is not the same as a human contribution record.

For Mountain West technology companies, the practical move is not to ban AI from R&D. It is to build an AI invention file before the demo becomes a financing, customer, or diligence conversation:

• the problem the team was trying to solve
• the humans who framed architecture, experiments, constraints, and design choices
• where AI generated options versus where people selected, modified, or reduced the idea to practice
• what is protected by patent, trade secret, contract, or speed
• whether employee, contractor, customer, and vendor IP terms support ownership

That file will not answer inventorship, patentability, or eligibility questions. But it gives counsel and leadership a better record to work from.

If AI is helping create product features, hardware designs, or technical processes, don’t wait until the patent filing or investor diligence request.

First step: run the AI Patent Strategy Scorecard, then turn weak spots into a 30-day invention-record cleanup plan.

## 3. Short Email / Newsletter Draft

**Subject:** The AI invention record investors may ask for later

**Preview text:** AI use is not the whole patent issue. The human contribution record is where leadership should focus first.

**Body:**

A growing number of technology teams are using AI during R&D: to generate alternatives, test approaches, summarize experiments, shape prompts, analyze data, or accelerate design work.

That does not automatically make the resulting work unprotectable. But it does create a practical recordkeeping problem.

If the company later pursues patent protection, raises capital, sells the business, or negotiates an enterprise deal, someone may ask a simple question: who actually contributed the inventive concept, and what role did the AI system play?

A useful starting point is an **AI Invention File**:

- the technical problem and product context;
- the human decisions that shaped the inventive path;
- the AI tools used and their role;
- employee, contractor, vendor, and customer IP ownership support;
- the initial patent / trade secret / contract protection map.

This is not a substitute for legal analysis, and it should not include confidential facts in a public form. But it can help leadership identify where the invention record needs cleanup before timing becomes urgent.
