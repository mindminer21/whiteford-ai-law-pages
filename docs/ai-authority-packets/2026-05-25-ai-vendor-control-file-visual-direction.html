The AI Vendor Control File

A board-ready record that connects AI tools to owners, data paths, contract rights, human review, and the evidence leadership may need for customers, buyers, investors, or regulators.

Leadership should be able to answer

  • Which AI vendors touch sensitive or customer-facing workflows?
  • Can the vendor train on company, customer, employee, or product data?
  • Where do contract rights, human review, and escalation actually live?
  • What evidence can the company produce during procurement or diligence?
