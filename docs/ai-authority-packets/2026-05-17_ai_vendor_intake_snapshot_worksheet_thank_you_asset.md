AI Vendor Intake Snapshot

A 15-minute, non-confidential worksheet for technology-company leaders to organize the first layer of AI vendor and use-case questions before product launch, enterprise sales, financing, board review, diligence, customer review, or AI governance planning.

Educational information only. Not legal advice. This worksheet does not create an attorney-client relationship and does not determine legal obligations or compliance status. Complete it internally using generic categories only. Do not submit confidential, privileged, trade-secret, vendor-specific, customer-specific, employee-specific, invention, deal, regulated, security-sensitive, or sensitive facts through a public form or email.

### Worksheet instructions

List each AI tool, AI-enabled vendor category, or AI use-case category in a separate row. Use generic labels such as “sales meeting summarizer,” “coding assistant,” “customer support chatbot,” “HR screening workflow,” “finance forecasting tool,” “product model feature,” “security monitoring platform,” or “data enrichment workflow.” Do not enter vendor names, customer names, contracts, prompts, outputs, employee facts, regulated facts, invention details, deal details, or confidential facts in any public submission.

## 6. Worksheet triage rubric

Use this after completing the seven fields. Do not treat the result as legal advice or a legal determination.

### Organize now

Mark a row “organize now” if any of the following are true:

- business owner is unknown;
- contract status is unknown or unclear;
- data category is unclear;
- vendor terms may affect confidentiality, training rights, output rights, audit, security, indemnity, or customer commitments;
- customer-flowdown obligations may apply;
- product launch, enterprise sale, financing, diligence, board review, procurement review, or renewal is approaching;
- no internal approval path exists.

### Monitor

Mark a row “monitor” if all of the following are true:

- business owner is known;
- data category is generic and low sensitivity;
- contract status is known at a high level;
- no near-term launch, diligence, customer, financing, employment, regulated-data, or consequential-decision trigger is present;
- the company has an internal process for periodic review.

### Pause for legal review

Mark a row “pause for legal review” if any of the following may be true:

- employment, lending, housing, health-care, insurance, legal-service, or other consequential-decision context;
- regulated, sensitive, employee, customer, product-output, security-sensitive, confidential, invention, IP, or trade-secret data;
- unclear vendor rights to train on inputs, use outputs, retain data, audit, subcontract, or change terms;
- customer-flowdown, enterprise-sale, security-review, diligence, or procurement commitments;
- Colorado AI Act, privacy, IP, employment, consumer-protection, security, export, contracting, or sector-specific concerns.
