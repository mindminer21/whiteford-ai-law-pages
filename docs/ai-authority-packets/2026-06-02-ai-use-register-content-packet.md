## 2. Polished LinkedIn post draft

AI governance gets much easier when leadership can see the AI use that already exists.

For many Colorado and Mountain West technology companies, the practical first step is not a sweeping policy. It is an AI use register: a short, maintained list of the AI tools, AI-enabled vendor features, and customer-facing AI functions the company is actually using.

The register should answer executive questions in plain English:

• Who owns the use case?
• What data flows into the tool?
• Could the output affect customers, employees, credit, hiring, safety, or regulated decisions?
• Which vendor terms control confidentiality, IP ownership, security, indemnity, and audit rights?
• What human review, escalation path, and evidence trail exists?

That register does not decide whether a particular use is compliant, patentable, or low risk. It does something more operational: it gives the CEO, CFO, GC, product lead, HR/risk owner, and board a shared map before vendor renewal, product launch, financing, or diligence.

Colorado’s AI framework is still moving, with key requirements extended to June 30, 2026. Companies do not need to freeze while the law develops. They do need to make AI use visible enough to govern.

## 3. Short email / newsletter draft

**Subject:** Before the AI policy, build the AI use register

**Preview text:** A practical executive map for vendor renewals, product launches, and Colorado AI readiness.

**Body:**

AI risk often becomes visible only after a vendor renewal, product launch review, customer security questionnaire, financing diligence request, or employee complaint.

A better starting point is an AI use register — a short operating list that shows leadership:

- where AI is being used,
- who owns each use case,
- what data flows into the tool,
- whether outputs could affect customers, employees, or regulated decisions,
- which contract terms govern confidentiality, IP ownership, security, indemnity, and audit rights, and
- what human review and evidence trail exists.

The register is not a legal conclusion. It is the map that lets executives prioritize legal review without freezing ordinary business use of AI.

For Colorado and Mountain West technology companies, this is especially useful while AI governance requirements continue to develop and the June 30, 2026 Colorado timeline approaches.
