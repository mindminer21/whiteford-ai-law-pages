Narrow diagnostic promise

First map the operating picture, then decide whether counsel needs the facts.

This prototype fulfills one narrow promise: help the team organize a non-confidential first-pass AI vendor/use-case picture before a product launch, enterprise sale, financing, diligence request, board discussion, or AI compliance conversation.

Not a compliance determination.

Use it to prepare better questions. Do not treat the snapshot as legal advice, a NIST requirement, a Colorado AI Act conclusion, diligence clearance, or a substitute for conflicts-cleared counsel review.

Complete value before follow-up.

The worksheet should stand on its own. Any later route to an AI Compliance Gameplan or Tech Company General Counsel review should come only after the diagnostic value has been delivered and the reader affirmatively opts in.
