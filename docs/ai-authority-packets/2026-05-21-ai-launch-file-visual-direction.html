Colorado + Mountain West technology companies

The AI Launch File

A board-ready legal operating layer before an AI feature reaches customers, enterprise procurement, investors, or diligence.

Design posture

Legal systems map, not a fake dashboard.

Use calm paper texture, squared cards, restrained violet pathing, and concrete operating records. The visual should feel like an executive file Jeff can walk through in a client conversation.

01 / OWNER

Use case + internal owner

What the system does, where it sits in product or workflow, and who maintains the record.

02 / FLOW

Data + model path

Inputs, outputs, retention, restrictions, and whether sensitive information is involved.

03 / TERMS

Vendor + contract controls

Confidentiality, IP ownership, security, audit, indemnity, and customer promise alignment.

04 / REVIEW

Human review + escalation

How exceptions, high-impact outputs, notices, explanations, and approvals are handled.

05 / IP

IP + evidence record

Invention notes, authorship signals, assignments, trade secret controls, and product history.

06 / SCREEN

Sensitive decision screen

Employment, finance, housing, insurance, education, health, or similar exposure before launch.

Who asks later?

Design for the predictable request list.

  • Enterprise procurementVendor, security, data, audit, and explanation questions.
  • Investor diligenceProduct risk, IP ownership, compliance posture, and records.
  • Buyer diligenceEvidence that AI use is controlled, assignable, and explainable.
  • Board / managementOwner map, escalation path, and readiness priorities.
  • Regulator or customerDocumented basis for how the company governs the use case.
