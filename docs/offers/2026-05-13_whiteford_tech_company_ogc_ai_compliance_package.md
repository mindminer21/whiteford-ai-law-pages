A fixed-monthly outside general counsel package for growth-stage technology, AI, robotics, SaaS, cybersecurity, data, and deep-tech companies that need practical legal infrastructure before they are ready for — or in addition to — a full-time general counsel.

**Core market message:**

> Practical outside general counsel for technology companies building AI-enabled products, enterprise software, regulated data workflows, or IP-heavy platforms — led by Jeff Schell on AI/IP/compliance strategy and supported by Whiteford attorneys across corporate, M&A, privacy, cybersecurity, employment, contracts, disputes, government contracts, real estate, tax, and financing.

## 4. Monthly retainer structure — $8,000/month

### Included monthly cadence

- **Monthly GC strategy call** — 60–90 minutes with Jeff and/or the assigned Whiteford relationship attorney.
- **Monthly legal operating plan** — a short prioritized action list: urgent, important, monitor, defer.
- **Office-hours access** — email/phone issue spotting for routine business/legal questions during the month.
- **Contract triage and negotiation guidance** — review and redline support for ordinary-course commercial agreements within the included monthly scope.
- **AI compliance and product-risk review** — led by Jeff, focused on AI governance, IP, product representations, vendor/customer contract implications, and practical compliance steps.
- **Specialist routing** — identify when employment, privacy, M&A, tax, litigation, government contracts, or real estate counsel should be involved.
- **Board/founder decision support** — concise issue memos or talking points for management/board discussions when needed.
- **Quarterly legal health review** — a structured review of contracts, IP, employment, privacy/AI, financing/M&A readiness, and open risk items.

### Included service posture

The retainer should be described as **managed access and recurring counsel**, not unlimited legal work. Suggested phrasing:

> The monthly retainer covers routine outside-GC guidance, issue triage, recurring strategy, and ordinary-course legal support. Larger transactions, litigation, financings, major employment disputes, patent prosecution, major privacy/security incidents, M&A, specialized tax work, or regulatory investigations may be scoped separately or handled by Whiteford specialists under separate engagement terms.

### Suggested included-hours framing, if needed internally

Externally, lead with outcomes. Internally, an $8,000/month package likely needs a budget assumption such as:

- roughly **10–15 hours/month** of relationship/OGC and routine support, depending on rates and staffing mix;
- specialist work included only as scoped triage unless expressly allocated;
- unused monthly time does not roll over unless Whiteford decides otherwise;
- emergency response, major transactions, litigation, patent filings, and regulatory matters are separately scoped.
