## 5. Approval flows

### Internal approval flow

Use for all posts before any external review.

1. Draft post in Social Planner or repo CSV.
2. Add internal note:
   - target audience
   - landing page / lead magnet
   - legal-risk flags
   - source video/article if repurposed
   - requested decision: approve, edit, reject, or hold
3. Send for approval to Jeff or assigned Whiteford approver.
4. Reviewer actions:
   - approve as a draft
   - edit caption
   - reject with comments
   - reschedule proposed time
5. Approval does not equal publication unless the approval packet says it does.

Suggested internal approval note template:

```text
Audience: <buyer role>
Category: <Social Planner category>
CTA: <landing page + lead magnet>
Legal/brand flags: <current-law check, Whiteford naming, attorney advertising, no guarantees>
Source: <YouTube brief, landing page, or original>
Requested decision: Approve draft / edit / hold. Do not publish unless separately authorized.
```

### External approval link flow

Use only when a non-HighLevel stakeholder needs to review posts without a HighLevel login, such as Whiteford marketing, a practice leader, or co-brand reviewer.

Rules:

- Generate external Magic Link only after Jeff approves the recipient and post set.
- Password-protect the link.
- Links expire after 30 days; track expiry.
- Desktop/web is the reliable approver experience.
- Revoke links when review closes or recipient changes.
- Do not send external links to prospects or clients for marketing collaboration unless Jeff approves the specific recipient and purpose.

External approval packet fields:

- recipient name and role
- post IDs/names
- purpose of review
- password delivery channel
- link expiry date
- revoke date
- final decision owner

## 8. Analytics cadence

Monthly analytics review: first business day of each month once channels are connected.

HighLevel path:

- Marketing > Social Planner > Statistics.

Metrics to review:

- posts published by category
- impressions/reach where available
- likes, comments, shares/engagement
- follower change where available
- top-performing posts
- CTA clicks where UTM or tracked links are available
- lead-magnet submissions by campaign tag in HighLevel
- content-to-lead mapping: Social category -> landing page -> trigger tag -> opportunity/meeting activity

LinkedIn limitations to note:

- LinkedIn Page analytics are supported more fully than LinkedIn Profile analytics.
- Post-specific statistics for LinkedIn Page are supported; LinkedIn Profile post-specific statistics may not be supported through HighLevel.
- Follower data can lag by n-2 days.
- Platform API limits and permissions can affect reporting.

Recommended UTM pattern:

`?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=ai_authority_2026&utm_content=<yyyy-mm-dd-category-topic>`

Example:

`/ai-patent-strategy-scorecard?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=ai_authority_2026&utm_content=2026-05-11-ai-patent-human-contribution`

Monthly decision questions:

1. Which category drove the highest qualified engagement?
2. Which landing page received the most social-assisted visits or submissions?
3. Which CTA language produced the most clicks without becoming hypey?
4. Which posts need legal-status refresh before reuse?
5. What should be drafted for the next 30 days?
