## 2. Dream customer / audience

Primary audience:

- Founder, CEO, CFO, GC, CTO, product/engineering executive, operations lead, or board-adjacent operator at a growing technology company in Colorado or the Mountain West.
- Company has real operating pressure but may not have a mature in-house legal function: enterprise sales, hiring/contractor scale, AI/data use, IP ownership questions, privacy/security questionnaires, financing, strategic partnership, or diligence.
- Buyer wants practical sequencing more than a broad legal lecture.

Dream-customer state:

- They know legal work matters but do not know where to start.
- They may have scattered contracts, IP records, vendor terms, AI/data practices, workforce records, and corporate approvals.
- They want a bounded, non-confidential, executive-readable way to decide which legal-operating category deserves first attention before the next business milestone.

Not the target for this lead magnet:

- A user seeking emergency legal advice, a dispute-specific answer, a privileged analysis, a definitive compliance determination, a patentability assessment, a financing-readiness opinion, a transaction-readiness opinion, or immediate representation.

## 3. Core pain and outcome

Narrow problem to solve:

> Which one legal-operating cleanup category should a growing technology company organize first before its next sales, hiring, financing, AI/data, enterprise-customer, or diligence milestone?

Pain:

- Legal operations feel scattered across inboxes, templates, counsel notes, finance folders, HR tools, engineering systems, and founder memory.
- Operators know “legal cleanup” is too broad to act on, so it gets deferred until a customer, investor, buyer, employee, contractor, auditor, insurer, or vendor asks for records.
- The company may not need a big memo; it may need a first visible workstream with an owner, records location, review trigger, stale-item list, and questions for later counsel review.

Promised practical outcome:

- A browser-visible legal-operating snapshot that identifies the readiness band, top cleanup category, and one 10-day non-confidential inventory action.
- A clear next step that can be done internally without submitting confidential facts.
- Optional report/email follow-up only if the user affirmatively opts in.
