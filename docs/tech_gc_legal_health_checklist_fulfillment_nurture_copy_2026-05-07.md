### Top-gap intro copy

### Category-specific top-gap modules

#### Legal operations

Top-gap label:

Legal operating cadence

Copy:

The company may benefit from a clearer rhythm for when legal review enters sales, hiring, product, governance, data, and financing decisions. A practical first step is to map recurring decision points, assign an internal owner, and identify when counsel review should be requested.

10-day action:

Create a simple legal operating map: decision type, owner, usual document, review trigger, approval path, and records location.

#### Customer and vendor contracts

Top-gap label:

Contract stack consistency

Copy:

The checklist suggests customer, vendor, NDA, partner, DPA, security, or order-form materials may not be managed as one operating system. A useful first step is to inventory templates, customer paper, fallback positions, renewal obligations, and approval thresholds.

10-day action:

Build a contract-stack inventory with document type, owner, last updated date, approval threshold, and known open questions.

#### IP ownership and innovation records

Top-gap label:

Ownership and invention records

Copy:

The company may need a clearer way to show who created core product work and where assignment, confidentiality, open-source, vendor, contractor, and invention records live. This is especially important before financing, customer diligence, patent strategy, or acquisition discussions.

10-day action:

Inventory founder, employee, contractor, advisor, vendor, and open-source records without adding confidential invention details to a public form.

#### Employment and contractor hygiene

Top-gap label:

Contributor documentation

Copy:

Hiring and contractor scale can create inconsistency if contributor documents are not current, centrally stored, and tied to onboarding/offboarding. The first step is usually not a broad HR overhaul; it is identifying which forms, roles, assignment language, confidentiality obligations, and records need review.

10-day action:

Create a contributor-record checklist for employees, contractors, advisors, and vendors who touch product, code, data, or customer relationships.

#### Privacy/security and customer assurance

Top-gap label:

Customer assurance readiness

Copy:

The company may need a clearer package for customer privacy/security questions. A practical starting point is to align the privacy policy, DPA or security exhibit, subprocessor/vendor map, questionnaire answers, and internal owner before a sales process compresses the timeline.

10-day action:

Assemble the current privacy/security answer set and mark which items are current, stale, missing, or awaiting review.

#### AI/data governance

Top-gap label:

AI/data review process

Copy:

AI and data tools can touch contracts, confidentiality, IP ownership, privacy/security, product claims, and records. The useful first step is an intake map: what tools are used, what data is involved, who owns review, what vendor terms apply, and when human review or escalation is needed.

10-day action:

Create an AI/data tool inventory by use case, owner, data type, vendor, contract status, review status, and records location.

Branch note:

If AI/data governance is a major pressure point, the AI Compliance Gameplan Builder may be a better follow-on diagnostic. Do not enroll the contact in that lane unless there is separate lane-specific opt-in or Jeff-approved compliant routing.

#### Corporate and financing readiness

Top-gap label:

Corporate records and approvals

Copy:

The company may have formation, equity, approval, financing, board/manager, and major-contract records in multiple places. Before financing or investor review, a practical first step is to centralize records and identify which approvals or documents appear stale, incomplete, or hard to locate.

10-day action:

Build a corporate records checklist with owner, document location, last update, and open questions.

#### M&A/diligence readiness

Top-gap label:

Diligence response readiness

Copy:

The checklist suggests that responding to a customer, investor, partner, or buyer diligence request may require reconstruction. A useful first step is a lightweight diligence folder organized by IP, contracts, corporate records, employment/contractors, privacy/security, AI/data, and product-claim support.

10-day action:

Create a diligence folder map with categories, owner, source location, and missing-item flags.

Branch note:

If financing or acquisition timing is the main pressure point, the Founder M&A Readiness Checklist may be a better follow-on diagnostic. Do not enroll the contact in that lane unless there is separate lane-specific opt-in or Jeff-approved compliant routing.
