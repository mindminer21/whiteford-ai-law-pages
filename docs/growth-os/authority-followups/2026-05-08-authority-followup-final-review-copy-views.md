1. customer MSAs, SOWs, security addenda, and order forms;
2. vendor and subcontractor terms;
3. AI, data, and security commitments;

5. privacy/security questionnaire answers and evidence files;

7. a repeatable process for updating those materials as the company scales.

For founders: legal readiness is often a sequencing problem, not a panic project.

For CFOs and operators: customer and investor questions are easier to handle when contracts, records, owners, and evidence live in one operating map.

For GCs and CTOs: AI, security, product, and legal commitments should be documented in a way the business can actually maintain.
