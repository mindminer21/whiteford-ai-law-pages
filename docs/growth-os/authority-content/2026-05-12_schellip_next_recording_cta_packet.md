## Topic 1 — AI Patent Brief

### Safe working title

AI Helped Build It. What Should You Document Before a Patent Review?

Alternate title options for attorney/SEO review:
- AI Patent Lawyer Question: What Was the Human Contribution?
- Can AI-Assisted Inventions Be Patented? Start With the Record
- AI-Assisted Invention Records: What Founders Should Organize Before Filing

### One-sentence hook

If AI touched the R&D process, the practical starting question is not whether AI was used; it is whether the team can explain the human technical contribution, ownership context, and supporting record before a patent-strategy conversation.

### 5-bullet recording outline

1. Start with the founder problem: AI-assisted development can create confusion around who contributed what, what should be documented, and what the patent strategy can safely rely on.
2. Separate tool use from the human contribution record: problem framing, technical choices, model/data decisions, architecture, validation, prototypes, and experiment results.
3. Explain the record categories to organize without sharing confidential facts publicly: invention notes, prompt/use logs where appropriate, commits, test results, meeting decisions, vendor/tool terms, and assignment documents.
4. Connect the record to strategy choices: inventorship review, patent-versus-trade-secret analysis, public-disclosure timing, fundraising/diligence narrative, and contractor/vendor ownership cleanup.
5. Close with a narrow diagnostic CTA: use the AI Patent Strategy Scorecard to identify documentation gaps before filing, fundraising, demo day, or a deeper legal review.

- Do not state that any AI-assisted invention is patentable, unpatentable, correctly inventored, eligible, owned by a particular person/entity, or safely fileable without counsel review.
- Public-form safety: do not ask viewers to post or submit confidential invention details, prompts, unpublished technical details, source code, claim charts, customer data, vendor contracts, or investor/deal facts.

Alternate title options for attorney/SEO review:

### One-sentence hook

When Colorado AI and ADMT rules are moving, companies should avoid both panic and paralysis by building a practical inventory of AI use cases, vendors, data inputs, decision impact, human review, records, and owners.

### 5-bullet recording outline

1. Open with the operational mistake: many companies jump to a generic AI policy before they know where AI is actually being used.
2. Show the inventory fields executives can gather without making legal conclusions: department, owner, tool/vendor, data category, output use, affected workflow, reviewer, and record location.
3. Flag higher-risk categories for counsel review: consequential decisions, employment/HR, finance/lending, housing, health, customer eligibility, regulated data, and external-facing product claims.
4. Add vendor/contract triage: data use, confidentiality, security, retention, deletion, audit, output rights, indemnity, and training-on-customer-data terms.
5. Close with the AI Compliance Gameplan Builder as a non-confidential way to turn the inventory into a readiness heat map and 30/60/90 workstream.
