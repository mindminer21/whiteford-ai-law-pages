## Pillar 1 — AI Patent Law

### One-screen summary

Working title: “AI-Assisted Invention: The Founder’s Human-Contribution Check”

Core diagnostic promise: help founders, CTOs, inventors, product leaders, CEOs, CFOs, and GCs identify whether an AI-assisted invention workflow has enough non-confidential human-contribution documentation to support a more focused patent-strategy conversation.

Plain-language premise: the useful first question is not simply “Did we use AI?” It is whether the human technical contribution, timing, ownership path, records, and disclosure posture can be explained without disclosing confidential invention details.

Core educational points:

1. Start with the human technical contribution, not the AI tool.
2. Separate AI assistance, prompts, model output, prototypes, and test records from any legal conclusion.
3. Map ownership before disclosure: founders, employees, contractors, advisors, vendors, open source, and tool terms may all affect the record.
4. Connect patent strategy to trade-secret controls and product-roadmap sequencing.
5. Choose a 10-day cleanup action: inventory non-confidential record categories, ownership documents, tool-use categories, and disclosure-timing questions.
