### Title options

1. AI-Assisted Invention: What Founders Should Document Before a Patent Review
2. The First AI Patent Question Is Not the Tool — It Is the Human Contribution
3. Before You File on an AI-Assisted Invention, Build This Record

### Thumbnail hook

“AI helped. Who invented?”

### One-sentence promise

This video helps founders identify the non-confidential records that can make an AI-assisted invention easier to discuss in a later patent-strategy review.

### 5–7 section recording outline

1. Start with the founder problem.
   - AI tools are now part of ordinary product and R&D work.
   - The legal-strategy question is not just whether AI was used.
   - The safer first question is whether the human technical contribution can be explained.
2. Separate tool use from contribution records.
   - Prompts, outputs, prototypes, tests, diagrams, and engineering notes may be part of the record.
   - Do not turn the public video into an inventorship conclusion.
3. Map the human technical contribution.
   - What problem did the team identify?
   - What architecture, design, experiment, tuning, or product decision came from people?
   - What changed because of human judgment?
4. Connect ownership and disclosure timing.
   - Founder, employee, contractor, advisor, vendor, open-source, and AI-tool terms may affect the record.
   - Public disclosure timing should be reviewed before promotional or fundraising materials reveal technical details.
5. Tie patent strategy to trade-secret and product-roadmap choices.
   - Not every differentiator belongs in a patent application.
   - Some information may need trade-secret controls, contract support, or product-roadmap sequencing.
6. Give the 10-day cleanup action.
   - Inventory non-confidential categories: documentation location, ownership records, AI-tool use category, disclosure timing, and pending questions.
7. Route to the diagnostic.
   - The scorecard gives structured educational triage before a strategy sprint is considered.

### Cold open draft

“If AI helped your team build a new feature, the first patent-strategy question is not simply, ‘Did we use AI?’ A more useful first question is: can the human contribution be explained without handing over confidential technical details? In this video, I’ll walk through the founder-friendly record you can start organizing before a patent-strategy review.”
