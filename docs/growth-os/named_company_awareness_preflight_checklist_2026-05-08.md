This checklist is for named-company awareness posts, prospect-linked CTAs, and related attorney-review packets. It is channel-agnostic, but optimized for LinkedIn awareness drafts where a named company may be referenced in educational/category-wide copy.

No email, SMS, DM, LinkedIn message, LinkedIn post, comment, tag, connection request, social post, newsletter, ad, HighLevel tag/task/note/opportunity/contact mutation, workflow activation, workflow test, workflow enrollment, Clio write, Supabase DDL or row mutation, public publish, production credential change, or other client/prospect-facing action is authorized by this template.

## Operating principle

Named-company awareness should earn trust before asking for attention. Use narrow, useful educational content and lead-magnet CTAs only when the reader can clearly understand that the post is category-wide, source-current, non-diagnostic, non-confidential, and not a statement about the named company’s legal condition. The business-development corpus was used only for synthesis: lead magnets should solve a narrow problem, build trust, and convert to owned opt-in traffic after value is earned.

## Use this template when

Use this preflight for any draft that does one or more of the following:

- Names a company, product, founder, executive, investor, customer, or financing event.
- Mentions a company in connection with a Whiteford/Schell IP offer lane, lead magnet, checklist, scorecard, YouTube hub, or consultation CTA.
- Includes or considers a company/person tag, handle, mention, or LinkedIn page reference.
- Uses public-source facts to support educational legal, AI, IP, compliance, contracting, M&A readiness, or outside-GC content.
- Links to a CTA route such as `/colorado-tech-general-counsel`, `/ai-patent-strategy-scorecard`, `/ai-compliance-gameplan`, `/colorado-ai-compliance-lawyer`, `/colorado-startup-ma-lawyer`, or `/ai-law-youtube`.

## Status labels

Use one of these labels before any manual-use decision:

| Status | Meaning | Allowed next step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `PASS_FOR_INTERNAL_REVIEW_ONLY` | Draft can be reviewed by Jeff/Whiteford internally. | Attorney/brand/source review; no external action. |
| `REVISE_BEFORE_FINAL_COPY` | Draft has useful structure but needs edits before exact-copy review. | Revise local draft; rerun this checklist. |
| `HOLD_NO_ACTION` | Targeting, evidence, clearance, or CTA is not ready. | Park in Agent Targeting/watchlist; no post/tag/outreach/live CRM action. |
| `READY_FOR_EXACT_APPROVAL_PACKET` | All preflight items are documented, but external action still requires Jeff/authorized approval of exact action. | Prepare an approval packet naming copy, URL, channel, timing, operator, evidence, and rollback. |

Default status for named-company LinkedIn awareness drafts is `PASS_FOR_INTERNAL_REVIEW_ONLY` or `HOLD_NO_ACTION`, not external approval.

## Decision shortcuts

Use these shortcuts to avoid over-approving incomplete drafts:

- If the source check is older than 48 hours for public use, mark `HOLD_NO_ACTION` until refreshed.
- If the draft relies on a source that cannot be opened, is paywalled-only, is social-only, or is not clearly tied to the current company/entity, remove the claim or mark `HOLD_NO_ACTION`.
- If conflicts, Clio, suppression/DND, or duplicate-prior-touch status is unavailable, mark `HOLD_NO_ACTION`.
- If the copy uses `@[Company - verify...]`, placeholder handles, or unresolved tag syntax, mark `REVISE_BEFORE_FINAL_COPY` or `HOLD_NO_ACTION` before any copy/paste view.
- If the CTA is a localhost, preview, or unapproved Vercel URL, mark `HOLD_NO_ACTION` for public use until the final production URL and UTM policy are approved.
- If the post suggests the named company has a specific legal problem, remove the implication or hold.
- If the channel is Jeff’s personal LinkedIn profile, treat the final post as a manual human action only. Do not schedule, automate, scrape, tag, comment, or DM from this checklist.
