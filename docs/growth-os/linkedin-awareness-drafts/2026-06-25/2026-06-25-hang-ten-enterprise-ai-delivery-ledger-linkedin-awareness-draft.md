## 7. Draft LinkedIn post for Jeff’s manual review

Draft only — attorney review required before any manual use. No legal advice. No attorney-client relationship. Not a comment on Hang Ten’s legal position, product readiness, customer posture, investors, or any company’s need for counsel.

> Enterprise AI does not just need a pilot plan.
>
> It needs a delivery ledger.
>
> Hang Ten Systems is a useful current signal. The company announced on June 24 that it raised a $32M seed round led by Mayfield, with Aramco Ventures as a strategic investor, to help large enterprises adopt AI and change how business-critical software is built, modified, and operated.
>
> The broader operator lesson is bigger than one company:
>
> When AI moves from demo to enterprise delivery, the hard part is not proving that something can be automated once.
>
> The hard part is showing what will keep working when the system touches real workflows, real data, real users, real vendors, and real business commitments.
>
> For any AI-enabled services rollout, I’d want a simple **enterprise AI delivery ledger**:
>
> 1. **Promise made** — what business result are we actually promising: speed, cost, accuracy, throughput, resilience, customer experience, or something else?
>
> 2. **Systems touched** — which core systems, integrations, workflows, and data stores are in scope?
>
> 3. **AI role** — what does the model or agent generate, rank, summarize, modify, decide, or route?
>
> 4. **Human role** — where does a person review, approve, override, test, escalate, or own the outcome?
>
> 5. **Change control** — how do updates to prompts, models, tools, workflows, vendors, and acceptance criteria get logged?
>
> 6. **Customer evidence** — what can the team show in procurement, board review, financing diligence, renewal, or post-incident review?
>
> 7. **Outcome metric** — how will the business know this improved something that matters?
>
> This is not about slowing down AI adoption.
>
> It is about giving the rollout enough operating memory that the buyer, builder, GC, CTO, and board are not relying on a slide deck and a heroic project lead six months later.
>
> If your company is buying, building, funding, or selling enterprise AI this quarter, build the ledger before the pilot becomes part of the business.
