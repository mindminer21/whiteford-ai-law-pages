## Ready-for-attorney-review LinkedIn draft

`@[Sereact - verify exact LinkedIn Page/tag]` is a useful signal for a broader physical-AI patent strategy issue:

Robotics companies are not just building machines that move.

They are building systems that perceive, decide, adapt, and operate in real-world environments.

That sounds like a patent question.

It is broader than that.

For physical-AI teams, the practical IP-readiness question is whether the company can keep a clean operating map of:

1. what was invented in the model, control system, workflow, and deployment stack,
2. what should be patented versus kept as trade secret,
3. what customer deployments may disclose,
4. what public demos, papers, hiring pages, and sales materials reveal,
5. what data rights support training, validation, and improvement,
6. how international and US expansion affect filing and disclosure timing, and
7. how the technical moat is explained to investors, partners, and enterprise customers.

For #founders: invention capture should happen while the technical roadmap is moving, not after commercialization creates a disclosure trail.

For #CTOs: patent strategy, trade-secret controls, demos, customer deployments, and technical records need one shared operating map.

For #investors: the defensibility story depends on documentation, not just technical sophistication.
