When AI, expert review, customer workflows, and product claims all move quickly, can the company explain what was actually invented, who contributed, what the model did, what the human experts did, and what evidence supports the roadmap?

For AI-native technical products, the invention record is no longer just a patent file. It is often a map of:

- human contribution,
- model/data/tool contribution,
- product architecture,
- evaluation and customer feedback,
- public disclosure timing, and
- what belongs in patents, trade secrets, contracts, or normal product documentation.
