## Ready-to-review LinkedIn copy — default no-tag version

AI-enabled drug discovery is a useful public reminder that “AI patent strategy” is not just about prompts or model outputs.

Enveda publicly describes an AI-enabled drug discovery platform, machine-learning work around natural-product chemistry, and a Boulder-based team advancing differentiated medicines.

That kind of public example points to a broader issue for AI/R&D companies generally — not a company-specific legal conclusion and not a comment on Enveda's legal position.

When AI is part of technical discovery, the useful internal questions often become:

1. What did the human team contribute?
2. Which data, models, assays, code, workflows, and lab records support the invention story?
3. What should be patent-facing versus kept as trade secret/process know-how?
4. Who owns each piece of the contribution record?
5. What can be discussed externally without disclosing confidential technical detail?

For founders and technical teams, the goal is not to over-lawyer every experiment.

It is to keep enough clean, non-confidential structure that later patent, partnership, diligence, and roadmap conversations are easier.

Educational starting point: AI Patent Strategy Scorecard.
