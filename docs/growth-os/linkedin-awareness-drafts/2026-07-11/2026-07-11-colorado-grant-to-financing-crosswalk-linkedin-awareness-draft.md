## 6. Draft LinkedIn caption for Jeff review

Colorado deep-tech founders: the grant application is not the real deliverable.

The reusable company record is.

Colorado OEDIT’s Advanced Industries Early-Stage Capital and Retention Grant window is open. Eligible projects can seek up to $250,000, with applications due August 27 at 5:00 p.m. MT.

The application asks teams to connect product and technology, business strategy and financials, company management, stakeholder support, and benefits to Colorado.

Founder translation:

Do not build five disconnected application sections.

Build one **grant-to-financing crosswalk** with five rows:

1. **Technical claim → proof**  
What is genuinely new? Which prototype, validation result, test, customer discovery, or technical record supports it?

2. **Commercial milestone → business result**  
What will the funded work make possible: a validated design, manufacturing step, pilot, regulatory milestone, customer decision, or scalable process? How will you measure completion?

3. **Use of funds → capital stack**  
Which grant dollars, matching cash, investor funds, federal funds, or revenue support each milestone? Which funds are committed, conditional, or still being raised?

4. **Workstream → accountable owner**  
Who owns the technical work, commercialization work, budget, reporting, partnerships, and hiring? What contractor, university, supplier, data, invention, and disclosure records travel with each workstream?

5. **Colorado benefit → operating dashboard**  
Which jobs, capital investment, facilities, suppliers, manufacturing activity, and commercialization outcomes should the company be able to report with evidence?

The useful test:

Could the same file support the grant reviewer, the next board update, and the first investor diligence request without changing the underlying story?

If not, the company probably has an application packet — not yet an operating record.

Educational only; not legal advice and not an attorney-client relationship. Confirm all eligibility, match, application, and deadline requirements directly with OEDIT.
