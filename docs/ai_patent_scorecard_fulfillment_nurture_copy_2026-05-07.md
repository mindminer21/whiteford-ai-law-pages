## 4. Documentation and ownership gap modules

Use “gap,” “record workstream,” “documentation priority,” or “strategy input” instead of definitive “risk” where possible. If “risk” appears in UI labels, define it as a triage signal rather than a legal conclusion.

### Gap-map intro copy

Your top gaps are the categories where your answers suggest the most record-building value may exist. They are not legal findings. Use them as a starting point for organizing non-confidential records, assigning owners, and deciding whether company-specific counsel review is needed.

### Gap-specific modules

#### Human contribution / inventorship record

Top-gap label:

Human contribution record

Copy:

AI-assisted R&D can move quickly enough that the team remembers the invention story but has not documented it in a reviewable way. A useful first step is to preserve non-confidential records that show what humans contributed, which technical choices mattered, and how the AI tool fit into the development process.

10-day action:

Create a non-confidential inventory of inventor/team roles, technical decisions, experiment logs, prototype milestones, and record locations. Do not include confidential invention details in public forms.

#### R&D evidence / notebooks

Top-gap label:

R&D evidence preservation

Copy:

Prompt, prototype, experiment, notebook, architecture, and testing records can become difficult to reconstruct after a demo, fundraising process, customer rollout, or employee/contractor transition. The practical first step is to preserve what exists and assign an owner for ongoing invention-record discipline.

10-day action:

Map record type, storage location, owner, date range, missing items, and whether the material contains confidential information that should only be shared after engagement.

#### Ownership and assignment records

Top-gap label:

Ownership chain and assignments

Copy:

AI patent strategy often depends on a clean ownership story across founders, employees, contractors, advisors, vendors, and open-source or third-party inputs. The scorecard cannot determine ownership, but it can flag whether the company should locate and review assignment and confidentiality records before a filing or diligence event.

10-day action:

Inventory contributor categories and document locations: founder IP assignments, employee agreements, contractor/advisor agreements, vendor SOWs, confidentiality terms, and open-source review notes.

#### AI vendor rights

Top-gap label:

AI vendor terms and output rights

Copy:

AI vendor terms may affect data use, confidentiality, output rights, training restrictions, indemnity, auditability, and product claims. The first step is not to assume the tool creates or destroys rights. It is to identify which tools touched R&D, what data or prompts were used at a categorical level, and which terms should be reviewed.

10-day action:

Create an AI vendor map with tool name, owner, use-case category, data category, contract/source of terms, last review date, and open counsel-review questions.

#### Technical differentiator / claim strategy

Top-gap label:

Technical differentiator clarity

Copy:

Patent strategy for AI-enabled software usually starts with the technical improvement, system architecture, data flow, model role, training/inference approach, performance change, or integration design. A useful record explains what changed technically without turning the public form into an invention disclosure.

10-day action:

Prepare a non-confidential technical-differentiator summary for intake, then keep confidential diagrams, code, experiment details, and claim ideas out of public tools until counsel engagement is in place.

#### Disclosure package and timing

Top-gap label:

Disclosure package readiness

Copy:

Public demos, pitch materials, customer conversations, publications, and product launches can create timing pressure. The scorecard does not determine legal consequences, but it can help the team identify whether a non-confidential package and disclosure-control plan should be reviewed before the next milestone.

10-day action:

List upcoming disclosure events, the materials involved, owner, expected audience, current review status, and whether a counsel-reviewed disclosure plan is needed.

#### Patent-versus-trade-secret posture

Top-gap label:

Patent/trade-secret decision path

Copy:

The strongest AI IP strategies rarely depend on one filing alone. Patents, trade secrets, contracts, vendor controls, confidentiality practices, product roadmap, and customer terms often need to work together. The scorecard can flag whether that decision has been queued for review, but it should not be treated as a filing recommendation.

10-day action:

Create a decision memo skeleton: business milestone, technical differentiator, expected disclosure pressure, records available, vendor/ownership questions, and counsel-review questions.
