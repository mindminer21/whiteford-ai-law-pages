# 90-Day @SchellIP AI Authority Recording Calendar

Planning window: May 11, 2026 through August 7, 2026. This gives 13 production weeks covering the first 90-day authority sprint from the May 5, 2026 assignment date, with the last recording week intentionally extending a few days to complete the full weekly cadence.

Sources and anchors used: Schell IP / Whiteford AI Authority and YouTube Growth Strategy in this repo; USPTO AI resources and 2025 revised inventorship guidance stating existing inventorship law applies to AI-assisted inventions and inventors must be natural persons; Colorado General Assembly SB26-189 page for ADMT/consequential-decision framing and January 1, 2027 introduced effective date; U.S. Copyright Office AI initiative page for digital replicas, copyrightability, and generative-AI training report topics. Treat current-law episodes as recording briefs requiring legal review before publication.

## Cadence and positioning

- Monday: AI Patent Brief, 8-12 minute solo explainer, primary CTA to the AI Patent Strategy Scorecard.
- Tuesday: Colorado AI Law Brief, 6-10 minute topical update, primary CTA to the AI Compliance Gameplan Builder.
- Wednesday: Founder Legal Operating System, 10-15 minute business-law explainer, primary CTA to the Colorado Tech Company Legal Health Checklist.
- Thursday: Deal-Ready Founder, 8-12 minute diligence issue spotter, primary CTA to the Founder M&A Readiness Checklist.
- Friday: buffer for legal/ethics review, thumbnail cleanup, Shorts extraction, YouTube description, newsletter, LinkedIn, and landing-page FAQ repurposing.
